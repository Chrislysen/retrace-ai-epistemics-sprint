# Source provenance and rights notices

This local research artifact preserves source attribution and mixed upstream notices. It does not place third-party content under a new blanket license. The detailed machine-readable inventory, exact source URLs, retrieval dates, hashes, transformations, selected passages, and qualifications are in `research/ecological-v6h-sources.json` and the captured notices under `data/ecological-v6h-sources/`. These are the record of verification at collection time; links may later change.

Dataset papers and exact upstream commits are listed in the inventory. Raw captures, selected source text, and authored administrative policies are separate layers. Policies, simulated withdrawals, and Boolean operationalization must not be attributed to the source authors. Public author names and bibliographic data occur in the source material; there are no private participant or patient-level records in these cases.

The earlier v5 artifact and archived development bundles retain their existing contents and notices. Historical snapshots are included for provenance, not as a claim that all archived material is current or independently cleared for publication. No bundled credentials, virtual environments, or third-party installed packages are needed to run the offline product. The software’s authorship record is preserved; this release does not invent a new open-source license on the author’s behalf.
