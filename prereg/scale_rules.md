# Rules for the scaling run (written before any of its model calls)

Written Monday 16 November 2026, about 05:25 CET, by the second AI session (Claude) with AI assistance. The author approved
the model calls in this session at about 05:15 CET: four models, at most 450 requests, a reservation ceiling of 35 credits.
This file and the generated cases are committed, and this file's SHA-256 is recorded in `prereg/HASHES`, before the first call.

## 1. Question

Does answering directly from prose get worse as the declared dependency structure grows, and does compile-then-compute hold up?
No direction is assumed. Every outcome is reported, including one in which compilation fails more than direct answering.

## 2. What had been seen when this was written

The first-day 24-case experiment (same prompts, about 10 records per case): gemma-4-31b-it fully correct on 12 of 24 from
prose and 22 of 24 through extraction; glm-53-flash 24 and 20. The 1,800-slot sweep, including that Gemma's direct verdict on
the 12 long-document cases was fully correct in 7 of 12 (short) and 1 of 12 (long) while its admitted declarations were exact
in 7 and 6. No output of the scaling run exists yet.

## 3. Cases

Generated by `post_sweep/scale_cases.py`, seed 20261116, in the schema of `data/prose-holdout.json`, with prose composed from
sentence templates copied from the first-day packets. Three sizes, 12 cases each (36 cases):

| Size | Records | Claims | Decisions | Records withdrawn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S | 10 | 5 | 6 | 1 |
| M | 20 | 10 | 8 | 2 |
| L | 40 | 20 | 10 | 3 |

About 40 percent of records are copies with one or two parents (chains allowed), about one record in ten is unavailable from
the start, claims have one to three alternative routes of one to three items, and an item may be an earlier claim. A generated
case is kept only if, after the withdrawal, at least two decisions are supported and two are not, at least one decision loses
support (two for M and L), at least one supported decision survives although it depends on a withdrawn record, and at least
one decision is unsupported from the start. Reference decisions come from the first-day engine (`retrace/core.py`) and must
agree with the independent evaluator in `post_sweep/recompute_prose24.py` on every case, or the case set is rejected.

## 4. Conditions, prompts and settings

Three conditions per case and model, built by the project's own `retrace/prose_sessions.make_prose_sessions` with its frozen
instructions, unchanged:

- `formal_fresh`: the formal ledger is given; the model returns the decisions.
- `prose_fresh`: the prose packet and the eligibility snapshot are given; the model returns the decisions.
- `prose_extract`: the same prose is given; the model returns the support graph, which the engine evaluates.

Runner: the project's first-day `scripts/run_adaption.mjs`, unchanged (temperature 0, no tools, no retry of any request).
Differences from the first day, fixed here: maximum output 8,192 tokens instead of 4,096 (larger graphs need longer output),
timeout 180 s, concurrency 2 within a model, at most two models at a time.

Models, in this order: gemma-4-31b-it, qwen3.8-27b, deepseek-v4-flash, glm-53-flash. 36 cases x 3 conditions = 108 requests
per model, 432 in all, plus at most one smoke request per model.

## 5. Failures and stopping

- The runner stops a segment at the first provider error. The failed request is not retried and counts as an unusable answer.
  Sessions not yet issued are run in a further segment, at most six segments per model.
- An unusable answer (failure, invalid output, rejected graph) counts every decision of that slot as wrong in the measure
  "over all scheduled decisions", as on the first day. It is also reported separately, because a refusal is not a wrong answer.
- All calls stop at 08:30 CET. Slots not issued by then are reported as not issued.

## 6. Outcomes

For each model, size and condition, out of 12 cases:

1. usable answers; fully correct cases; decision errors over all scheduled decisions;
2. among usable answers: unsafe decisions (answered supported, reference unsupported) and dropped decisions (answered
   unsupported, reference supported);
3. for `prose_extract`: graphs accepted by the validator, graphs identical to the reference, accepted graphs with a wrong decision.

Comparison of interest, per model and size: `prose_extract` against `prose_fresh`, as fully correct cases and as the paired
difference in decision accuracy over all scheduled decisions, with a percentile interval from 5,000 resamples of the 12 cases
(seed 20261116). A second paired comparison uses only usable answers and unsafe decisions. No significance threshold is set:
12 cases per cell support description, not a test.

Scoring is done twice: with the project's frozen `retrace/prose_eval.score_prose` and with the independent evaluator in
`post_sweep/recompute_prose24.py`. Any disagreement between the two is reported.

## 7. Labels

This run is post hoc relative to the sweep and prespecified relative to its own outputs. It uses the first-day engine and
graph schema (records, copies, alternative routes, decisions), not the multi-reading declarations of the main engine, and
generated prose, not natural documents.
